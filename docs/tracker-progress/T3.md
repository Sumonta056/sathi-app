## Hand test (user)

1. Run the app with tracker on. Press option+command+Space in another app: B opens. Change the shortcut in settings: the old one stops, the new one works.
2. Lock the screen (control+command+Q), wait 20 s, unlock: the Away starts at the lock time.
3. Sleep the Mac (Apple menu, Sleep), wake: Away starts at the sleep time. Close the lid, reopen: same.
4. Leave the Mac untouched for the idle time (set 10 min): Away starts at the last input, not at the 10 min mark.
5. Calendar: tick one calendar to read, make a meeting, check it is found; delete an activity with an event: the event goes; untick the calendar: nothing is read.
6. `./scripts/perf.sh`: wakeups under 10 a minute while idle.
