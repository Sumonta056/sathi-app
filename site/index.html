Free macOS menu bar app

Drink water, read, and breathe, with a little help.

Sathi lives in your Mac menu bar. Three friendly buddies remind you to drink water, read, and meditate. They stay gentle, and they get firmer only if you keep saying "later".

Meet the buddies

Three small habits, three small friends

Fota water

A reminder every 2 hours between 9:30 and 18:30. Log a few sips, half, or a full glass. The daily goal is 8 glasses.

Boi reading

A calm popup in the top-right corner at 22:00. It never takes focus from your work. Start a 15-minute timer, and Boi counts the minutes.

Shanti meditation

A guided breathing session in the morning, afternoon, or evening. Pick box, relax, or even breathing. Off by default.

You can change every time, interval, goal, and weekday in Settings.

Features

Kind reminders that respect your focus

A water reminder you can answer in one click

Fota asks, you answer. Pick how much you drank, or snooze for 5 minutes.

  • Log a few sips, half a glass, or a full glass
  • Use a sip bottle and drag its water line instead
  • No answer in 5 minutes counts as a snooze

A reading popup that never steals focus

Boi waits quietly in the corner while you finish your sentence.

  • Shows on the weekdays you pick
  • The timer runs in a small pill in the same corner
  • Skips the day when you already met your goal

Guided breathing, a few minutes at a time

Shanti breathes with you and counts for you. Pick the time slots that fit your day.

  • Box, relax, or even breathing patterns
  • Morning, afternoon, and evening slots
  • An optional soft chime

Reports that show your week at a glance

See each habit by day, week, or month. Keep your streak green.

  • Average per day and percent of your goal
  • A bar chart with your daily goal line
  • A streak calendar, your best day, and your longest streak

Settings that fit on one screen

Each buddy has its own page. Turn a buddy on or off with one switch.

  • Interval, active hours, container, and daily goal
  • Pick how firm each buddy is: Gentle, Nudgy, or Strict
  • Open Sathi when you log in

Reminders

Gentle first. Firmer only if you ask for it.

Each buddy has a strictness level. If you ignore a reminder, it escalates step by step.

Gentle

A reminder each time. It never stays on top.

Nudgy

After 1 snooze, the reminder stays on top and wiggles.

Strict water only

After 2 snoozes, a full-screen lock asks you to drink. Hold a button for 3 seconds to unlock. An emergency skip is always there.

Need quiet time? Pause all reminders for 30 minutes, 1 hour, or the rest of the day from the menu bar.

Privacy

Your habits stay on your Mac

Sathi makes no network requests. There is no account, no analytics, and no cloud. Your data is plain JSON that you can read.

~/Library/Application Support/Sathi/

prefs.jsonYour settings
state.jsonLive state, such as the bottle level
history/YYYY-MM.jsonEach done, snooze, and skip, kept forever

  1. Open the DMG

    Drag Sathi to the Applications folder.

  2. Open Sathi the first time

    Sathi is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks first. Open Sathi once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can also right-click Sathi and choose Open.

  3. Find the drop in your menu bar

    Click it, then the gear icon. On the General tab, turn on Open Sathi when I log in.

  4. Try a reminder now

    In Settings, pick a buddy and click Show a reminder now.

FAQ

Questions

Is Sathi free?

Yes. Sathi is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, no subscription, and no ads.

Does Sathi send my data anywhere?

No. Sathi makes no network requests. Your settings and history stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Sathi/ on your Mac.

Which Macs can run Sathi?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on Apple silicon or Intel.

Why does macOS say it cannot check Sathi for malware?

Sathi is not notarized by Apple. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway next to Sathi. You do this once.

Can Sathi lock my screen?

Only for water, and only if you pick Strict. After 2 snoozes a full-screen lock asks you to drink. You unlock it by holding a button for 3 seconds, and there is always an emergency skip. Reading and meditation never lock the screen.

Does Sathi slow down my Mac?

No. Sathi uses less than 1% CPU when idle, under 40 MB of memory, and has no repeating 1-second timers.
